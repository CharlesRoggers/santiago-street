# World design (§6–§9, §27, §42)

First district: **La Cancha** (fictional). Main court *Plaza del Cerro* (20×32, lit, hub);
secondary court *Pasaje Once* (16×26, hidden, unlit). One fictional metro station on
*Línea Naranja* of the fictional *Línea Urbana* network. Content lives in `packages/content`.

Hybrid generation: procedural street furniture/repetitive buildings; hand-authored courts,
landmarks, hero streets. Streaming per ADR-004. Santiago cues: hills, plazas, dense mid-rise
housing, overhead wiring, commercial galleries, warm evening light, cool night with sodium /
LED floodlights.
