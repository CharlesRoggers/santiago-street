# Intellectual property policy (§4, §5, §74, §75)

Never use: real clubs, players, sports/clothing/shoe/beverage brands, ad campaigns, league or
tournament names, official metro branding, copyrighted music, ripped or extracted game assets,
copied textures, Street View or commercial map imagery, identifiable real graffiti or one-to-one
copies of private landmarks.

Always: fictional equivalents (brands: VANTA, RUSH, KICKR, NEXO, STREET9 — placeholders to be
trademark-checked), original graffiti library, fictional metro (`Línea Urbana`, colours and
signage of our own), fictional landmarks inspired by urban character only.

Every third-party asset is logged in `asset-licences.md` with source, licence and date.
Crew logos: curated library only, no uploads (§72).
